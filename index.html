✦ About

Beneath the Lie is a mystery thriller romance novel about secrets, betrayal, and the weight of old memories. When Elara receives a cryptic message about a night she swore to forget, she is pulled back into a web of lies that three years ago tore her world apart. As she digs deeper, she discovers that the truth is far more dangerous than she ever imagined — and that some people will do anything to keep it buried.

Beneath the Lie • Aman Fatima
