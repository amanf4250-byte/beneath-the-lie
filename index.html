The Story

Some memories never really disappear.

Elara believed she knew the truth. Three years ago, everything changed. A murder. A disappearance. A story everyone believed. Then Roman returned. Alive. Successful. And carrying answers that could destroy everything Elara thought she knew. Now she has to decide what is more dangerous... the truth, or the people who spent years hiding it.
